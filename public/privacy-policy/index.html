Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, profile details, posts, comments, messages, donation amount, receipt references, device/browser information, and support messages.

Sharing, cookies, and storage

We may share necessary information with trusted service providers such as payment gateways, hosting providers, analytics tools, email/SMS providers, and support tools. We use essential cookies and browser storage for login security, preferences, offline support, and basic analytics.
