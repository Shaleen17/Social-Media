Refund and Cancellation Policy

Donations are voluntary. Refunds are reviewed fairly for duplicate payments, wrong amounts, accidental payments, technical errors, or other genuine issues.

Effective and last updated: May 22, 2026

Cancellation before payment

You may cancel a donation before completing payment by closing checkout or not authorizing the transaction. Once payment is successful, the request will be handled as a refund request.

When refunds may be considered

  • Duplicate payment for the same donation.
  • Incorrect amount entered by mistake.
  • Payment deducted but donation confirmation failed because of a technical issue.
  • A request that Tirth Sutra, after review, considers fair and genuine.

When refunds may be declined

Refunds may be declined for change of mind after a voluntary donation, requests made after funds are allocated or used, missing transaction details, or suspicious/fraudulent activity.

How to request a refund

Email tirthsutra@gmail.com with your name, registered email or phone, payment date, amount, Razorpay/payment ID if available, and reason for the request.

Review and timeline

We aim to review refund requests within 3 to 7 working days. Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method. Bank and payment gateway timelines may vary.
